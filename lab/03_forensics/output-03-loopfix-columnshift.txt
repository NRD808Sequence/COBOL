THE ONE-COLUMN QUESTION - record sequential vs. a newline-delimited file
Same binary, same logic, two physical file formats.

### run A: authentic card image, 120 bytes, no delimiters

The FD declares a 20-byte record and the SELECT has no ORGANIZATION
clause, so reads are record sequential: fixed 20-byte slices with no
notion of a line. Record 1 lands correctly. Record 2 begins on the
newline byte, and every record after it is shifted one column left.
The newline is the anachronism, not the COBOL.
